## 1. Bug Fix: compute_share.py (num_batches_tracked)

**Problem:** 
um_batches_tracked (a BatchNorm running counter, value ≈7820 after 20 training
epochs) was included in the norm_delta calculation. Since each residual block has 2 BN layers,
this added 2×7820 = 15640 to every block's delta, making all four blocks appear identical.

**Fix:** Skip keys ending in 
um_batches_tracked, unning_mean, and unning_var before
computing deltas (these are non-learnable buffers, not parameters).

## 2. K-Shard Recovery Curve (n=6)

Script: scripts/eval_kshard_curve.py
Results: esults/raw/kshard/kshard_curve_n6.json

An attacker substitutes k shards of the repaired model with the corresponding shards from the
infected model. We measure ASR (%) — how much of the backdoor is recovered.

**ASR (%) by number of substituted shards:**
| k | repair-aware | architectural | random (avg 5 draws) |
|---|-------------|---------------|----------------------|
| 0 | 0.90        | 0.90          | 0.90                 |
| 1 | 0.50        | 1.02          | 2.67                 |
| 2 | 4.57        | 1.24          | 5.68                 |
| 3 | **92.89**   | 13.67         | 17.93                |
| 4 | 97.11       | 25.97         | 46.46                |
| 5 | 97.21       | 97.21         | 72.69                |
| 6 | 97.43       | 97.43         | 97.43                |

Repair-aware order: [3, 4, 2, 1, 0, 5]

**Key finding:** The repair-aware strategy crosses the 50% ASR threshold at k=3
(shards 3+4+2 collectively account for 93.5% of total share_s). The architectural
strategy does not cross 50% until k=5. This confirms that an attacker who targets
high-share_s shards recovers the backdoor with significantly fewer substitutions.
