## What the numbers mean now

With repaired ASR confirmed at 0.90%, the substitution results from 16/09 are now valid. The baseline was clean. The reason single-shard rollback failed is not a bad baseline — it is because clean fine-tuning distributes the repair across all layers. No single layer group retains enough backdoor information to restore the attack on its own.

## Chinh's conclusion (from his 18/09 note)

Repair method: **clean fine-tuning**. This causes the infected weights to be overwritten globally, not locally. As a result, swapping any one shard back from the infected model introduces weights that are incompatible with the surrounding repaired layers — the backdoor signal cannot propagate end-to-end.
