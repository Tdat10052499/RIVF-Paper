## Section 4.1 — r_s and share_s (20-epoch clean fine-tune)

### n = 3

|-------|--------|-----|---------|
| 0 | conv1, layer1 | 0.20051282 | 0.25000000 |
| 1 | layer2, layer3 | 0.20051283 | 0.50000002 |
| 2 | layer4, linear | 0.20051282 | 0.24999999 |

### n = 6

| Shard | Layer | r_s | share_s | norm_delta |
|-------|-------|-----|---------|------------|
| 0 | conv1 | 0.15833991 | ≈ 0 | 0.58 |
| 1 | layer1 | 0.20051282 | 0.25000000 | 15640.00 |
| 2 | layer2 | 0.20051282 | 0.25000000 | 15640.00 |
| 3 | layer3 | 0.20051282 | 0.25000000 | 15640.00 |
| 4 | layer4 | 0.20051282 | 0.25000000 | 15640.00 |
| 5 | linear | 0.08649614 | ≈ 0 | 0.37 |

**Key finding:** The 20-epoch global fine-tune concentrates ~100% of its update in the 4
residual blocks (layer1–4), split equally at 25% each. conv1 and linear are virtually
unchanged (norm_delta < 1). This explains why single-shard rollback fails: no single
shard carries more than 25–50% of the repair update.
